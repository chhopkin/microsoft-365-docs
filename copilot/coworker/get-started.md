# Get started with the Coworker agent

Coworker is an agent available in Microsoft 365 Copilot. You describe what you need — draft an email, build a spreadsheet, schedule a meeting — and Coworker handles it. This quickstart walks you through your first conversation, from sending a request to reviewing the result.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot access** — An active Microsoft 365 Copilot license assigned to your account.
- **A modern browser** — Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome recommended.
- **The Coworker agent available** — Coworker is enabled in your Microsoft 365 Copilot environment.

## Open Coworker

1. Go to **Microsoft 365 Copilot** in your browser.
1. Select the **Coworker** agent.

You land on the Coworker home page. You'll see a chat input where you can describe what you need, along with any recent tasks you can pick up where you left off.

## Start your first conversation

1. **Type what you want done** in the chat input. You can enter up to 16,000 characters, so feel free to include plenty of detail.
1. **Attach files** if needed — drag and drop files onto the input area, or use the file picker to browse.
1. **Pick a specific agent** by typing **@** and selecting from the list (optional).
1. **Use voice input** by selecting the microphone icon to speak your request instead of typing (optional).
1. **Send** your message.

> [!TIP]
> The more specific you are, the better your results. Instead of "send an email," try "Send an email to the marketing team summarizing last week's campaign results and include the attached report as a PDF."

## Watch Coworker work

After you send your message, Coworker begins processing your request. Here's what you'll see:

- **A thinking indicator** appears while Coworker figures out the best approach.
- **Skill messages** let you know when Coworker picks up a new skill it needs — like Word, Excel, Email, or Scheduling.
- **Tool steps** show exactly what Coworker is doing at each stage, such as "Composing your email" or "Creating your presentation."
- **The response streams in** in real time, so you can follow along as Coworker builds your result.

Each step appears in the chat as it happens.

## Approve actions

Before Coworker takes an action on your behalf — like sending an email or scheduling a meeting — it asks for your permission. When the approval dialog appears, you have three choices:

- **Approve** — Allow this action to proceed.
- **Approve and remember** — Allow this action, and skip the prompt for similar actions in the future.
- **Cancel** — Stop this action.

> [!NOTE]
> Coworker does not take actions without your approval. You can also **pause** Coworker at any time (softly to finish the current step, or immediately), **resume** when you're ready, or **cancel** the task entirely.

## Review your results

When Coworker finishes, any files it created appear in the **side panel** on the right. From there you can:

- **Download** files to your device.
- **Preview** files directly in the browser — supported formats include PDF, Markdown, images, CSV, and HTML.

The side panel also shows overall progress and the skills Coworker used. You can give a **thumbs down** on responses that missed the mark, or a **thumbs up** on ones that were helpful.

## Find your past work

Select the **Tasks** view to see everything you've asked Coworker to do:

- Switch between **list view** and **kanban board** depending on how you like to organize.
- Select any task to open it and resume the conversation.
